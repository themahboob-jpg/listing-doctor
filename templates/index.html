      What does the score actually mean?
      
          0–100 across up to 11 rule-based checks covering listing completeness and potential issues. The score is a prioritization aid, not a prediction of sales or search ranking. Checks eBay's API can't show are marked “not scored”.
          
    
          
    
          
      Is it really free?
      
          Yep. Free, no signup, no credit card. Paste a link, get your diagnosis.
          
    
          
    
          
      Where does the data come from?
      
          Live eBay data via the official eBay API. (If API keys aren't connected, you'll see clearly-labeled demo data instead.)
          
    
          
    
          
      Do you save my listing?
      
          We don't keep your listing or tie it to you — reports are cached for a few minutes for speed, then forgotten. Nothing is stored long-term.
          
    
          
    
          
      Only eBay?
      
          eBay for now. Etsy, TikTok Shop and Walmart are next on the roadmap. 🗺️
