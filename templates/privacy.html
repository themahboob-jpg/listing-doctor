Privacy Policy

Effective: October 13, 2026

TL;DR: Listing Doctor collects as little as possible. No accounts, no cookies, no third-party trackers, no data sales. The details:

Listings you audit

When you paste an eBay URL or item ID, we send it to eBay's official API to fetch that listing's public data (title, price, photos, specifics, shipping, and so on). Our server analyzes it and returns your score and fixes. The report is cached in server memory for about 10 minutes for speed, then discarded. We don't store your audited listings long-term, and we don't tie them to you.

Email — only if you ask

The "Send me the report" form is optional. If you use it, your email address — plus your score, listing title, and listing URL — is sent to Formspree, the form service we use to deliver the email. That's the only time your information goes to a third party. See Formspree's own privacy policy at formspree.io.

Technical bits

  • Rate limiting: to keep the tool free and abuse-free, we count requests per IP address in server memory. These counters expire automatically.
  • No cookies, no trackers: we don't set cookies and we don't embed third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Security: traffic is encrypted (HTTPS) and standard security headers are set on every response.

What we never do

We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't share data with anyone — except Formspree, and only when you explicitly ask for the report email.

Children

Listing Doctor is a tool for sellers and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above and note material changes here.
