Privacy Policy

Effective: October 31, 2026

TL;DR: Listing Doctor collects as little as possible. If you allow it, we use Google Analytics to understand site usage. No accounts, no advertising, and no data sales. The details:

Listings you audit

When you paste an eBay URL or item ID, we send it to eBay's official API to fetch that listing's public data (title, price, photos, specifics, shipping, and so on). Our server analyzes it and returns your score and fixes. The report is cached in server memory for about 10 minutes for speed, then discarded. We don't store your audited listings long-term, and we don't tie them to you.

Share links

"Share my score" creates a link containing your score and the listing title (up to 70 characters), plus a signature that stops anyone editing the score. Nothing is stored; the link itself carries that information.

Technical bits

  • Rate limiting: to keep the tool free and abuse-free, we count requests per IP address in server memory. These counters expire automatically.
  • Google Analytics — only with your consent: Google Analytics 4 is not loaded until you click "Accept" on the cookie notice. If you accept, it may collect usage and browser/device information and use cookies or similar identifiers; Google processes this data under its own policies, and we don't use it for advertising. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set. Your choice is saved in your browser; clear this site's data to be asked again.
  • Security: traffic is encrypted (HTTPS) and standard security headers are set on every response.

What we never do

We don't sell your information or show ads, and we don't share it with anyone except Google Analytics (only if you accept) and eBay (the listing ID you audit).

Children

Listing Doctor is a tool for sellers and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above and note material changes here.
